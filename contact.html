Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge for site measurement and survey?
No, we provide site measurement surveys and design catalog consultations completely free within our service area.
What is the typical delivery timeline for safety gates?
Standard iron safety gates are fabricated and installed within 7 to 10 working days, while custom designer gates with CNC laser cutting may take 12 to 15 days.
Do you offer anti-rust warranty on stainless steel railings?
Yes, we use SS 304 and SS 316 grade premium stainless steel which comes with lifetime rust resistance and premium high-gloss polishing.
What material options are available for roof sheds?
We install Color-Coated Galvanized Sheets, Polycarbonate Sheets for transparency, and heavy-duty GI pipe frames for strong structural support.
